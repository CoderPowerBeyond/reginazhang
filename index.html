Research agendaDirections for an independent group

My group will develop reusable algorithms for evolving graphs and validate them where the stakes are high, beginning with neurodegenerative disease.

  1. Aim 1

    Forecasting disease progression on brain networks

    Forecast regional tau accumulation in Alzheimer’s disease from longitudinal PET, using brain connectivity as structure, accounting for scan quality, and producing calibrated uncertainty validated across cohorts.

  2. Aim 2

    Individualized models of progression

    Integrate imaging with genomic information so that forecasts reflect the individual patient rather than the population average.

  3. Aim 3

    Reusable methods and rigorous benchmarks

    Release algorithms and benchmarks that show when robust and efficient graph learning helps, how it transfers across domains, and what it costs.
